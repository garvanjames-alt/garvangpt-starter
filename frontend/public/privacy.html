Privacy Policy

Almost Human (GarvanGPT) • Last updated: 2025‑11‑22

This policy explains what information the Almost Human app (the “App”) collects, how it’s used, and your choices. We keep this simple and honest while the MVP is live.

1. What this App is

Almost Human provides educational information about medicines and health topics through an AI assistant. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

2. What data we collect

We may collect:

  • Chat messages you type and the assistant’s replies.
  • Technical data needed to run the service (e.g., IP address, device/browser type, basic logs).
  • Optional admin “Memories” content added by the site owner to improve answers.

We do not intentionally collect special categories of personal data (like medical records). Please avoid entering highly sensitive personal or health information.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide and improve the App.
  • To keep the App secure and prevent abuse.
  • To debug errors and maintain performance.

4. AI processing

Your messages are sent to an AI model provider to generate responses. The provider may process data on our behalf to return an answer. We do not sell your data.

5. Cookies

The App uses essential cookies for login/session security in the admin area. These cookies do not track you for advertising.

6. Data retention

We keep chat/server logs only as long as needed to operate and improve the App. Logs may be deleted periodically. You can request deletion of your data by emailing support@almosthuman.ie.

7. Your choices

  • Don’t share sensitive personal or health details in chat.
  • You may request access or deletion of your data by contacting us.

8. Children

The App is not intended for children under 16. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we’ll remove it.

9. Contact

Questions? Email: support@almosthuman.ie

MVP note: This policy will be refined as the product grows, but we’ll always keep it clear and human‑readable.
